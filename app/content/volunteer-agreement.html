WODDI Volunteer Leadership Agreement

Between you — the volunteer leader — and the Women of Divine Destiny Initiative (WODDI), "The Nurturer".

1. The nature of your service

Every leadership position in WODDI is voluntary. There is no salary and no stipend. You serve because you believe in raising women and young women across Africa, and because the service itself develops you as a leader. Nothing in this agreement creates employment, agency, or partnership between you and WODDI.

2. Your commitment

You agree to give the time your role genuinely requires and to be honest about your availability; to follow through on responsibilities even when no one is watching; to stay engaged through your network's regular rhythm of meetings and activities; and to complete the 14-day Activation Journey and the trainings your role requires within the published programme window.

3. Character and conduct

You agree to uphold WODDI's values and the Volunteer Leaders' Code of Conduct at all times; to treat members, fellow leaders, and communities with respect and dignity; to protect confidentiality; and to disclose any conflict of interest promptly to your leadership.

4. Safeguarding above all

You agree to put the safety and wellbeing of members — especially young women and minors — above all else; to never ignore or mishandle a safeguarding concern; to follow the reporting procedure and escalate immediately; and to keep all interactions visible, accountable, and appropriate.

5. Representing WODDI

You agree to use your correct network title — Representative in WGMN, Lead in WNNN — and to represent WODDI truthfully. You will not speak for WODDI to media, government, or partners, or enter commitments on WODDI's behalf, without authorisation from your leadership.

6. Information and member data

Any member information you access through your role — names, contacts, records in the WODDI systems — is entrusted to you for service only. You agree to use it solely for WODDI purposes, to keep it confidential, and never to copy, sell, or share it outside the organisation. This duty continues after your service ends.

7. Materials and access

Accounts, documents, group administration rights, and materials provided for your role remain WODDI's. When your service ends, you agree to hand over access and materials promptly and in good order.

8. Duration and ending of service

Your service continues while you hold your role and remain in good standing. Either you or WODDI may end the volunteer relationship at any time, with communication and respect. WODDI may reassign, pause, or conclude roles as the mission and its safeguarding standards require.

9. Recognition

Because roles are unpaid, WODDI honours service through recognition, development, and belonging — certificates and digital badges, free access to WODDI Institute learning, a continental network of peers, and a clear path of growth. Consistent service is seen, valued, and rewarded.

10. Acceptance

By pressing Accept below, you confirm that you have read and understood this Agreement and the Code of Conduct, and that you enter volunteer leadership service with WODDI freely and wholeheartedly. If you press Decline, your activation pauses until you are ready — speak with HQ if anything here needs clarification.
