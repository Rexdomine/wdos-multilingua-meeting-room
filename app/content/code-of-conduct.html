VOLUNTEER LEADERS’ CODE OF CONDUCT

The standards of conduct expected of every WODDI volunteer leader

Applicable to leaders of the Good Mother Network and the Nurture NextGen Network

Governance Policy Document

Women of Divine Destiny Initiative · Abuja, Nigeria · thewoddi.org

1. Purpose and Commitment

This Code of Conduct sets out the standards of behaviour expected of every volunteer leader serving in the WODDI Good Mother Network (WGMN) and the WODDI Nurture NextGen Network (WNNN). It exists to protect the women and young women WODDI serves, to safeguard the integrity and reputation of the organisation, and to give every leader a clear and shared understanding of what it means to lead with honour.

WODDI — The Nurturer — exists to empower women, strengthen families, nurture young people, and uplift communities across Africa. Because leaders act in WODDI’s name and are trusted by the members they serve, their conduct must reflect the values of the organisation at all times.

2. Scope and Application

  • This Code applies to all volunteer leaders at every level of both networks — Country Representatives and Country Leads, their Deputies and Assistants, State or Regional and LGA or District leaders, Chapter Leads, Chapter Communication Leads, and Campus Ambassadors.

  • It applies to conduct in all WODDI settings: meetings and convenings, chapter and circle activities, digital platforms and group communications, programme delivery, and any occasion where a leader represents or is identified with WODDI.

  • It applies from the moment a leader is appointed and for as long as they hold a leadership role.

  • Accepting a leadership role means accepting this Code. Every leader is expected to read, understand, and abide by it.

3. Core Values Every Leader Upholds

The conduct expected of leaders flows from the values WODDI lives by. Every leader is expected to embody these values, not merely to avoid breaching rules.

3.1 Integrity

Leaders act honestly and consistently, whether or not they are being observed. They keep their commitments, tell the truth, and do not misrepresent themselves, the organisation, or its work.

3.2 Respect and dignity

Leaders treat every member, colleague, and community member with courtesy and respect, valuing the dignity of every woman and young woman regardless of background, belief, status, or circumstance.

3.3 Service

Leaders serve the members and communities in their care, placing the good of others above personal advantage, recognition, or convenience.

3.4 Accountability

Leaders take responsibility for their actions and decisions, report faithfully, and accept correction with humility.

3.5 Care and protection

Leaders put the safety and wellbeing of members — especially young women and minors — above all other considerations.

4. Standards of Expected Conduct

Every WODDI leader is expected to:

  • Uphold WODDI’s values, mission, and this Code of Conduct at all times.

  • Treat all members, fellow leaders, and community members with respect, patience, and fairness.

  • Lead by example in character, communication, and commitment.

  • Give the time and attention their role genuinely requires, and be honest about their availability.

  • Follow through on responsibilities faithfully, whether or not anyone is watching.

  • Communicate promptly, clearly, and courteously through official channels.

  • Submit reports accurately, honestly, and on time.

  • Represent WODDI truthfully and protect its name and reputation.

  • Respect the leadership structure, reporting lines, and decisions of the organisation.

  • Handle any WODDI resources, funds, or property honestly and account for them fully.

  • Support and encourage fellow leaders, and resolve disagreements respectfully.

  • Continue to grow through WODDI’s learning and development opportunities.

5. Safeguarding and the Protection of Members

Safeguarding is the highest duty of every WODDI leader. Because both networks serve women and young women — and WNNN serves young women and, in some settings, minors — the protection of members is never optional and is never outweighed by any other consideration.

5.1 Every leader must

  • Place the safety, wellbeing, and dignity of members above all else.

  • Keep all interactions with members visible, appropriate, and accountable.

  • Follow WODDI’s safeguarding reporting procedure and escalate any concern immediately.

  • Protect the confidentiality of members’ personal information.

  • Obtain the appropriate consent before sharing images, stories, or personal details.

5.2 No leader may ever

  • Ignore, conceal, delay, or mishandle a safeguarding concern.

  • Be alone with a minor in a manner that is not visible and accountable.

  • Exploit, harm, intimidate, or abuse any member in any way — physically, emotionally, sexually, financially, or spiritually.

  • Share a member’s personal information or images without proper consent.

6. Prohibited Conduct

The following are serious violations of this Code. No purpose, pressure, or circumstance justifies them.

  • Any form of abuse, exploitation, harassment, intimidation, or discrimination against a member, colleague, or community member.

  • Any breach of safeguarding — including ignoring, concealing, or mishandling a concern.

  • Dishonesty, falsification of records or reports, or misrepresentation of WODDI or its work.

  • Misuse, theft, or unaccounted handling of WODDI funds, resources, or property.

  • Soliciting or accepting money, gifts, or favours in exchange for membership, position, or advantage.

  • Unauthorised fundraising, financial requests, or commitments made in WODDI’s name.

  • Entering partnerships or making agreements in WODDI’s name without proper authority.

  • Using a WODDI position for personal, political, or commercial gain.

  • Sharing confidential information, member data, or internal communications without authority.

  • Conduct that brings WODDI’s name into disrepute, whether online or offline.

  • Bullying, factionalism, or undermining fellow leaders or the leadership structure.

7. Conflict of Interest

A conflict of interest arises where a leader’s personal interests could improperly influence, or appear to influence, their WODDI responsibilities.

  • Leaders must disclose any actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest to their coordinator or the appropriate authority as soon as it arises.

  • Leaders must not use their position to benefit themselves, their family, or their associates unfairly.

  • Where a conflict exists, the leader must step back from the relevant decision and allow it to be handled transparently.

  • Disclosure is always the right course. A disclosed conflict, handled openly, is not a breach; a concealed one is.

8. Digital Conduct and Communication

Much of WODDI’s work happens through digital platforms and group communications. Leaders carry the same standards of conduct online as in person.

  • Use official channels for official communication, and keep group communications respectful and values-aligned.

  • Share only accurate information, and never spread rumour, misinformation, or unverified claims in WODDI’s name.

  • Protect login details, member data, and internal materials, and never share them without authority.

  • Represent WODDI with dignity on personal social media where a leader is identified with the organisation.

  • Never use WODDI platforms to promote personal, political, or commercial interests without approval.

9. Representation, Resources and Finances

  • Leaders represent WODDI only within the authority of their role and must not speak or commit on the organisation’s behalf beyond it.

  • Any handling of funds or resources must be transparent, properly recorded, and fully accounted for.

  • No leader may solicit funds, make financial commitments, or enter agreements in WODDI’s name without written authorisation from the appropriate authority.

  • Leaders must confirm who is authorised to sign agreements and approve commitments, and must not exceed that authority.

10. Breaches of This Code

A breach of this Code is treated seriously and in proportion to its nature. The purpose of addressing breaches is to protect members and the organisation, to correct conduct, and to uphold the standards every leader has accepted.

10.1 How breaches are handled

  • Any person may report a suspected breach to the appropriate coordinator or leadership authority.

  • Concerns are reviewed fairly, confidentially, and without prejudgement.

  • The leader concerned is given a fair opportunity to respond, except where immediate protective action is required.

  • The outcome is proportionate to the breach and communicated to the leader concerned.

10.2 Possible outcomes

  • Guidance, correction, or additional support and mentoring.

  • A formal warning.

  • Suspension from the role while a matter is reviewed.

  • Removal from the leadership role.

  • Referral to the appropriate authorities where a matter is unlawful.

11. Affirmation

By serving as a volunteer leader of WODDI, each leader affirms that they have read and understood this Code of Conduct, that they accept the standards it sets, and that they will uphold them faithfully throughout their service.
